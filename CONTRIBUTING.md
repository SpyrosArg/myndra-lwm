# Contributing to MYNDRA-LWM

MYNDRA-LWM is a living framework built on the observation
that world model systems introduce a class of security
vulnerabilities that existing frameworks do not address.
It is a starting point, not a final answer.

Contributions are welcome in three forms.

## New attack categories

If you have identified an attack surface in world model
systems that is distinct from the ten entries in this
taxonomy, open an issue describing:

- Which cognitive component it targets
- Why it is mechanistically distinct from existing entries
- At least one concrete real-world scenario
- Proposed detection and mitigation approaches

New entries are reviewed for technical accuracy and
distinctness before merging.

## Corrections and refinements

If you find a technical error, an imprecise description,
or a detection or mitigation approach that is incomplete
or incorrect, open an issue or submit a pull request with:

- The specific entry and section
- The correction and your reasoning
- Any supporting references

## Real-world examples and validation

Documented incidents, published research, or empirical
results that validate, refine, or challenge an existing
attack category are especially valuable. Open an issue
or pull request with the supporting material and how it
maps to the relevant WM entry.

## Guidelines

- Precision over coverage. A well-defined entry for a
  narrow attack is more valuable than a broad entry that
  conflates distinct mechanisms.

- No vendor promotion. Mitigation references should point
  to techniques and approaches, not specific commercial
  products.

- Cite your sources. Claims about attack feasibility or
  mitigation effectiveness should reference published
  research or documented incidents where possible.
